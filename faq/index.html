Tuning

Tuning & performance

How much power will I gain?

It depends on the car, fuel and parts. We'll give you realistic expectations for your exact setup before we start. See our B58 and S58 guides.

Is tuning safe for my engine?

A good tune stays inside safe limits for your hardware and fuel. We check the car's health and log it before and after.

Will tuning void my warranty?

Modifying engine software can affect warranty coverage on related parts. We'll go through the trade-offs for your car before any work.

Where can I buy parts?

From us, or from our stores: B58 Racing for B58 parts and GB Motors Store for parts, detailing products and fragrances.

Detailing

Detailing & ceramic coating

Do I need paint correction before ceramic coating?

Usually, yes. A coating locks in whatever is underneath, so swirls and scratches should be corrected first. We'll inspect the paint and recommend the right level.

Is ceramic coating worth it in Dallas?

Texas sun, heat, bugs and hard water are rough on paint. A coating helps resist UV fading, etching and water spots, and makes the car much easier to wash.
