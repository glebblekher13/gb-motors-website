Flagship project

The 1,500 HP B58

Our shop car is a B58-powered build headed toward 1,500 horsepower and chasing records. Every part and calibration strategy we offer customers gets tested here first.

  • Why the B58: it's the engine we know best, and the heart of our sister brand B58 Racing.
  • Why 1,500: to find where the limits really are, so customer cars stay far from them.
  • Follow the build: @thegbmotors on Instagram and YouTube.
