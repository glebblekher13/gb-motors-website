The process

Clean. Correct. Protect.

Wash

Safe hand wash, wheels and door jambs.

Decontaminate

Iron and tar removal and clay, so the paint is truly clean.

Correct

Multi-stage machine polishing to remove swirls and scratches and bring back gloss.

Protect

Ceramic coating locks it in against UV, heat, bugs and water spots.

Why ceramic in Dallas

Built for Texas heat

Dallas sun, heat, bugs and hard water are tough on paint. A ceramic coating adds a hard, slick layer that resists UV fading, etching and water spots and makes the car much easier to keep clean. Read more in the press.
