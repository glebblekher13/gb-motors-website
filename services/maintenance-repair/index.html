What we do

Common European car maintenance

Every car and model year is different. We use the factory schedule as a baseline and adjust for how you drive.

OIL

Oil service

Full-synthetic oil and a new filter, plus a look underneath for leaks. Turbo engines are hard on oil, so regular service matters.

BRAKES

Brakes

Pads, rotors, sensors and brake fluid. Fluid absorbs moisture over time, which hurts pedal feel and performance.

COOLING

Cooling system

Water pumps, thermostats, hoses and expansion tanks. Plastic cooling parts age with heat, and Texas summers are tough on them.

IGNITION

Ignition

Spark plugs and coils are wear items. A weak coil or old plug can cause misfires, rough idle and lost power.

FLUIDS

Fluids and filters

Coolant, transmission and differential fluid, plus engine and cabin air filters. Small items that protect expensive parts.

CODING

Diagnostics and coding

Fault codes, live data and electrical faults. Many new parts, like batteries, need to be registered or coded to the car.

Before you tune

Maintenance comes first

A tune makes a healthy engine stronger, not a tired one. Extra boost finds weak parts fast.

That's why every ECU tune starts with a health check and baseline logs. If something needs attention, we show you with photos and handle it here first.

  • Leaks: oil and coolant leaks get fixed before adding power.
  • Ignition: fresh plugs and healthy coils keep a tuned engine running clean.
  • Boost path: we check charge pipes and intake parts for cracks.
  • Fluids: clean oil and coolant help with the extra heat.

Platform guides: BMW B58, S58 M3 and M4, S55 M3 and M4 and VW Golf. Planning parts? See performance upgrades. Want it clean too? Ask about detailing.

Questions

Maintenance & repair FAQ

What cars do you service?

European cars: BMW, Audi, Porsche, Mercedes-Benz and Volkswagen. BMW is home base, and we work on stock and modified cars.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, we work by appointment. Book online or call us, and we'll set a time that works for you.

How do I know what work my car needs?

Every car gets a digital inspection with photos and video sent to you. We explain what we found and what can wait, and you approve the work before we start.

Do you follow the factory maintenance schedule?

We use the factory schedule as a baseline. For tuned cars, cars that see track days or cars driven hard in Texas heat, we may suggest checking some items more often.

Can you diagnose a warning light or check engine light?

Yes. We use dealer-level scan tools to read fault codes and live data, then track down the cause before replacing parts.

Should I service my car before a tune?

Yes. A tune makes a healthy engine stronger, not a tired one. We start every tune with a health check and baseline logs, and handle anything that needs attention first.
