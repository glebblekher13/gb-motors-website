About GB Motors

A Dallas shop built by car people, for car people. Founded in 2025 and grown by the local car community.

The shop

GB Motors is a European performance shop in Dallas, specializing in BMW and the rest of the European lineup: Audi, Porsche, Mercedes-Benz and Volkswagen. We focus on ECU tuning and performance upgrades, with maintenance and repair and detailing to keep those cars right.

We live by one rule: if it doesn't make the car faster, and it hasn't been proven on our own builds, we don't sell it. The shop is the test lab, social media is the storefront, and our online stores are where it all comes together.

GB Motors has grown mostly through social media and the Dallas car community. Our first car meet drew so many people that the lot ran out of parking.

From the court to the shop

Before cars, Gleb Blekher was a professional tennis player. Born in Moscow, he represented Canada as a junior, reached a career-high ITF world junior ranking of No. 129, earned an ATP ranking, and played NCAA Division I tennis at Northwestern University, graduating in 2024 with a degree in Economics.

He founded GB Motors in Dallas in 2025 and acquired B58 Racing in 2026. The competitive drive is the same, just with more horsepower.
