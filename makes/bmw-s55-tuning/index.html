Which cars

The S55 M3 and M4

The S55 is BMW M's twin-turbo inline-six with motorsport-grade internals. It powered the F80 M3 sedan and the F82 coupe and F83 convertible M4.

It makes big gains from software alone, and with the right supporting parts it goes much further. Turbo limits, fueling and heat decide what makes sense at each stage, so we plan the build around your goals, not a generic file.

Fuel

Pump gas, ethanol and flex fuel

Fuel is the single biggest factor in what an S55 can safely make. A stage 1 tune is commonly set up for 93 octane pump fuel, which is what most Dallas drivers can buy anywhere.

Ethanol blends add power and knock margin, which helps a lot in a Texas summer. The catch is that the fuel system has to keep up with the blend. On stock fueling we'll recommend a blend your car can actually support. For higher ethanol content and bigger power, we plan port injection or other fuel upgrades as part of the build, and tune for the blend you'll really run.

Not sure what's in the tank? Tell us how you fill up and we'll set the tune around it.

Before we tune

Older cars, done right

Many F80 and F82 cars are now several years old, and a tune makes a healthy engine stronger, not a tired one. Every job starts with a health check and baseline logs, and every car gets a digital inspection with photos and video.

  • Leaks and cooling: we check for oil and coolant leaks before adding power.
  • Ignition: plugs and coils are wear items, and a tune exposes weak ones fast.
  • Boost path: we inspect the charge pipes and intake side for cracks and leaks.
  • Crank hub: we'll talk through it before any higher-power build.

If something needs attention first, we'll show you why and handle it through our maintenance and repair service.

Questions

S55 M3 & M4 FAQ

Which cars do you tune on the S55?

The F80 M3 and the F82 and F83 M4. We'll confirm your engine and software from your VIN before we quote.

What's the first mod for an S55 M3 or M4?

A tune. The S55 responds strongly to software on a stock car, and a good stage 1 sets up the rest of the build.

What fuel should I run?

Stage 1 tunes are commonly set up for 93 octane pump fuel. Ethanol blends add power and knock margin, as long as the fuel system can support the blend. We'll tune for the fuel you can actually buy.

My M3 or M4 is older. Is it ready for a tune?

Maybe. These cars are getting older, so we start every tune with a health check and baseline logs. If something needs attention first, like a leak, a worn ignition part or a cracked charge pipe, we'll show you with photos before any work.

What about the S55 crank hub?

It's one of the most discussed topics on the S55, especially as power goes up. We'll talk through your power goals and the options before we build a higher-power car.

Can I go back to stock?

On most cars, yes. Your original software can be saved and flashed back. Ask us about your specific car.

Do you tune S55 cars remotely?

Yes. If you can't come to Dallas, we can work through remote tuning with log reviews and revisions.

Tuning availability and results depend on model year, software and hardware. Modifying engine software can affect warranty coverage on related parts, and some modifications are intended for off-road or competition use only. We'll confirm what's possible for your exact car before any work.
