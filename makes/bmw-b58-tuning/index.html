Gen 1 vs Gen 2

Know which B58 you have

Gen 1 cars run from roughly 2015 to 2019. The Gen 2 (TU) engine that followed changed a lot under the skin.

  • Fuel system: Gen 2 runs much higher injection pressure, with new injectors and a bigger fuel pump.
  • Turbo and head: Gen 2 comes in two layouts, one for cars like the M340i and Supra and one for cars like the 540i. Turbos and tunes differ.
  • Newer cars: some 2022+ engines add factory port injection.

We confirm your engine and software from the VIN before we quote.

Toyota

The GR Supra 3.0

The Supra 3.0 uses the same B58 as the M340i, with the same strengths and the same limits.

Most published B58 dyno results come from Supras and M340i cars. A 540i or X5 40i starts lower from the factory, so expect a little less from the same stage.

Fuel

Pump gas, ethanol and flex fuel

Stage 1 is commonly set up for 93 octane.

Ethanol adds power and knock margin, which matters in a Texas summer. Stock fueling supports a partial blend, not full E85, and Gen 1 cars run out sooner. For more, we plan fuel upgrades and tune for the blend you'll really run.

Ethanol can dilute the oil, so E-blend cars need shorter oil change intervals.

Before we tune

A healthy engine first

Every job starts with a health check and baseline logs, and every car gets a digital inspection with photos and video.

  • Leaks and cooling: we check for leaks and watch oil temperature on hot days.
  • Ignition: tuned cars usually want a colder plug with a tighter gap.
  • Charge pipe: the factory plastic pipe is known to crack under more boost.
  • Knock: sustained torque and knock hurt B58s, not peak horsepower.

Anything that needs fixing first goes through our maintenance and repair service.

Transmission

The ZF 8HP and big power

B58 cars with an automatic use a version of the ZF 8-speed. It's tougher than its paper rating, but stock units are commonly reported to start slipping around 650-700 whp.

A transmission calibration can raise clamping pressure and torque limits. For big-turbo cars we plan an upgraded transmission as part of the build, not after something fails.

Questions

B58 FAQ

Gen 1 or Gen 2 B58: does it matter?

Yes. The Gen 2 (TU) engine has different internals and software, so tunes and some parts differ. We'll confirm which one you have from your VIN.

What's the first mod for a B58?

A tune. Most B58s gain a lot from software alone, and it sets you up for the rest of the build.

Do you tune the GR Supra?

Yes. The Supra 3.0 uses the B58, so it gets the same tuning and parts expertise as our BMWs.

What fuel should I run?

Stage 1 tunes are commonly set up for 93 octane. Ethanol blends add power and knock margin, but the stock fuel system can't support full E85. We'll tune for the fuel you can actually buy.

How much power can a stock B58 take?

It depends on generation, fuel and use. The stock turbo is commonly reported to top out around 550 whp on E85. Beyond that, fuel, transmission and internals all need a plan.

Will my transmission hold the power?

The stock ZF 8-speed is commonly run at stage 1 and stage 2. For big-turbo power we plan transmission upgrades as part of the build.

Where can I buy B58 parts?

Our sister brand B58 Racing sells B58 performance and cosmetic parts that ship from Texas. We can install them too.

Tuning availability and results depend on model year, software and hardware. Modifying engine software can affect warranty coverage on related parts, and some modifications are intended for off-road or competition use only. We'll confirm what's possible for your exact car before any work.
